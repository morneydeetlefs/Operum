Getting Started
Welcome to Operum
Operum is a field-first industrial operations platform. It connects your asset register, your people, and your condition monitoring tools into one system — built for the plant floor, not the office.
All roles
What Operum does
Every function in Operum connects back to the asset register — the master list of every piece of equipment on your site. From there, safety inspections, maintenance work orders, and condition monitoring readings all know exactly which machine they belong to.

How to sign in
1
Open the app
Go to operum.pages.dev/app on any device — desktop, tablet, or phone. The app works in any modern browser without installation.
2
Enter your email and password
Your site administrator will give you your login credentials. Use the email address registered against your employee record.
3
Your role loads automatically
The app shows only the modules and actions your role permits. You will not see options that are outside your access level.
Your session stays active until you sign out. On shared devices, always sign out when you are done.

Register Module
Employee Register
The employee register is where all platform users are created and managed. Every person who uses Operum — from artisans to site admins — has an employee record that defines their access.
Admin Supervisor Safety Manager Maint. Planner
Viewing employees
Open the Register module and tap the Employees tab. All active employees are listed with their role badge. Inactive employees are hidden by default.
Employee list — demo
Adding an employee
Only Admin users can create, edit, or deactivate employee records.
1
Tap + Add in the Employees tab
The Add Employee form slides up from the bottom of the screen.
2
Set the Employee ID
Choose a short, memorable ID — for example emp_042 or jsmith. This ID is permanent and cannot be changed after creation.
3
Assign a role and areas
Select the appropriate role from the dropdown. In the Areas field, enter the area IDs this person is assigned to — for example bay_a, bay_b. Use all for unrestricted access.
4
Tap Save Employee
The record is created immediately. The employee can sign in using their email address once a password is set.
Deactivating an employee
Tap any employee row to open their record, then tap Edit. Deactivation is a soft delete — the record and all its history is preserved. The employee can no longer sign in but their historical data remains intact for audit purposes.
Never delete an employee record. Deactivate instead. Historical records — readings, inspections, sign-offs — must remain traceable to the person who performed them.
Register Module
Asset Register
The asset register is the spine of Operum. Every machine, component, and measurement point on your site lives here, organised into a tree that mirrors the physical layout of the plant.
All roles
How the tree works
Assets are organised as a hierarchy — a tree. Each node can have child nodes at any depth. The full ID of any node encodes its complete location in the plant.

Navigating the tree
1
Tap a node row to select it
The detail card appears below, showing the node's ID, criticality, hazards, isolation points, and actions. Measurable nodes show a CM badge — DiagnosticWand readings can be taken there.
2
Tap the › chevron to drill into children
The chevron on the right side of any row navigates one level deeper. The breadcrumb at the top tracks where you are.
3
Use the breadcrumb to go back up
Tap any level in the breadcrumb — All Sites › SITE-01 › 03 CV — to jump back to that level instantly.

Adding a node
Admin, Supervisor, Safety Manager, and Maintenance Planner roles can add and edit nodes. Artisans and Operators can view the tree but cannot modify it.
1
Navigate to the parent level first
Drill into the node you want to add a child under. Or stay at root level to add a new top-level site.
2
Tap + Register child node
The dashed button at the bottom of the tree list opens the Register Node form.
3
Use the ID suffix suggestion dropdown
Click into the ID Suffix field. A dropdown appears with suggested suffixes — the system looks at existing siblings to detect patterns (e.g. if GB-1 and GB-2 exist, it suggests GB-3 at the top) and shows the full industrial library ordered by most-used on your site.
Suffix suggestion — demo
4
Fill in the label, type, and criticality
The label is a plain English name for the node. Criticality — Critical, High, Medium, Low — determines how the node is highlighted in dashboards and alerts.
5
Add hazards and isolation points
List known hazards as comma-separated values — for example electrical, rotating, high-temp. Isolation points are the physical lockout points for this asset — for example ISO-01, ISO-02. These feed directly into Safe Work Procedures.
6
Tick Measurable if readings apply
If DiagnosticWand vibration or acoustic readings should be taken at this node, tick the Measurable checkbox. A gold CM badge will appear on the node in the tree. You can enable or disable this at any time as long as no readings exist.

Deleting a node
A node can only be deleted if it has no children and no documents attached. To delete a branch of the tree, you must delete from the bottom up — deepest children first, working back to the parent.
Nodes with readings attached cannot be deleted. This protects the integrity of the historical condition data.
Condition Module
Condition Monitoring
Condition monitoring connects Operum's asset register to DiagnosticWand — the vibration and acoustic analysis tool. Any node marked as Measurable in the asset register can receive readings from the field.
Admin Supervisor Maint. Planner Artisan Read Only
What is a measurable node?
Any node in the asset tree can be marked as measurable — not just machines. A bearing (component), a specific measurement position (maintainable), or an entire gearbox assembly can all be measurable independently.

Safety Module · Phase 2
Safety Management
The Safety module covers all OHSA-mandated safety functions — from daily toolbox talks to formal Permit to Work. It is currently in design and will be available in Phase 2.
Safety Manager Supervisor Artisan
What is planned
The Safety module will include the following, built in sequence from lowest to highest risk:
  • Toolbox talks — attendance register, topics covered, sign-off per person
  • BBS observations — behaviour based safety capture in the field
  • Safety rep inspections — Section 8/9 OHSA legal requirement
  • Incident & near-miss reporting — Section 24 OHSA compliance
  • Risk assessments — linked to job cards and asset records
  • Permit to Work (PTW) — highest stakes, built last, requires safety officer input before design begins
All safety functions are connected to the asset register. A toolbox talk is linked to the assets being worked on. A risk assessment references the asset's known hazards and isolation points. Nothing is free-floating.
Maintenance Module · Phase 3
Maintenance Management
The Maintenance module replaces paper job cards and informal verbal handovers with a structured digital workflow — from fault notification through to sign-off and feedback.
Maint. Planner Supervisor Artisan Operator
What is planned
  • Fault notifications — any user can raise a fault against an asset
  • Work orders — created from notifications or planned schedules
  • Job cards — artisan-facing, mobile-first execution view
  • Planned maintenance scheduling — PM frequencies linked to asset records
  • Sign-off and feedback loop — close the loop from fault to fix
  • Maintenance history per asset — full record of all work done
Condition monitoring data from DiagnosticWand feeds directly into maintenance decisions. A WARNING or CRITICAL reading on a measurable asset will eventually auto-generate a fault notification.
